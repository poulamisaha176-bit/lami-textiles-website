MATERIAL 01 // BIO_SEQUINS
Developed from fish scales, these bio-sequins explore iridescence without plastics.
Made using natural binders and natural, protein-based dyes, they retain the organic shimmer of the scale while remaining fully biodegradable. Each piece reflects light differently, capturing the idea of ornamentation rooted in sustainability and circular design.
BaseDiscarded fish scales
ColourNatural & protein-based dyes
FinishNaturally iridescent
End of lifeFully biodegradable
ApplicationsEmbellishment, embroidery, surface
A direct alternative to the synthetic sequin — the single embellishment most responsible for fashion's microplastic load.
