ABOUT
A material-led response to ocean microplastic pollution — transforming fish scales, a widely discarded marine by-product, into biomaterials for a climate-conscious textile future.
Rooted in the fishing communities of Bengal and developed in London, the practice treats waste as a design brief: every material begins at the market stall and ends as sequin, leather, paper or composite.
