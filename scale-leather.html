MATERIAL 02 // SCALE_LEATHER
Created from fish-scale fibre and natural polymers — a flexible sheet with the tactility of leather, fully bio-based.
Its surface is soft, durable and stitch-friendly, designed for accessories and interior applications where texture and strength meet low-impact materiality. Dyed with food and floral waste, each hide carries its own depth of colour, from warm amber to deep oxblood.
BaseFish-scale fibre + natural polymers
ColourNatural food & floral waste dyes
FinishSoft, matte, subtly grained
End of lifeFully biodegradable
ApplicationsAccessories, interiors, stitched goods
Soft, durable and stitch-friendly — a hide grown from what the kitchen leaves behind.
